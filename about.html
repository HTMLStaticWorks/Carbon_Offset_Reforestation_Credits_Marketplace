Our Story

Reimagining the concrete jungle.

Mission & Vision

Our mission is simple: to weave natural ecosystems into the fabric of urban environments. We envision cities where rooftops and walls are not just structural necessities, but living, breathing contributors to the local ecology.

Every project we undertake is a step towards reducing urban heat islands, improving air quality, and promoting biophilic design that enhances human well-being.

Our Journey

From a small botanical design firm to industry leaders in living architecture.

2015: The Seed

Founded by a group of passionate botanists and structural engineers with a single pilot project in a small urban courtyard.

2018: Growth Spurt

Successfully completed our 100th living wall installation and expanded our services to include intensive green roofs.

2021: Smart Integration

Launched our proprietary IoT smart irrigation system, setting a new standard for low-maintenance vertical gardens.

2026: Greening the Future

Recognized globally for sustainable urban design. We continue to innovate and expand our ecological footprint.

Guiding Principles

Our Ecological Philosophy

Every blueprint we draft is governed by five non-negotiable commitments to ecological integrity and durability.

Native Biodiversity

We restore native pollinator pathways by reintroducing local flora, sheltering bees, hummingbirds, and beneficial insect fauna.

Circular Materials

Our drainage cells are pressed from 100% post-consumer recycled ocean polymers, achieving net negative lifecycle carbon.

Zero Runoff Priority

Every installation serves as an urban sponge, buffering torrential stormwater and preventing combined sewer overflow events.

Radical Telemetry

No guesswork. We continuously audit soil moisture, ambient temperature, and carbon sequestration with open client dashboards.

Our Botanical Laboratory

20 Acres of High-Stress Plant Cultivation

Before a plant enters an urban rooftop, it spends up to 18 months acclimating in our climate simulation greenhouses.

Located in the Skagit Valley, our experimental research nursery subjects flora cultivars to rapid freeze-thaw cycles, high wind simulation, and simulated prolonged droughts. Only cultivars that maintain 99%+ vitality are approved for client architectural installations.
