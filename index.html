Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about structural feasibility, maintenance, and return on investment.

Can my existing roof support a living green roof without reinforcement?

In most cases, yes! Our extensive sedum systems weigh between 15 and 28 lbs per square foot when fully saturated with water, which is well within standard structural tolerances for modern commercial and residential buildings. During our preliminary site audit, our licensed structural engineers calculate exact weight capacities and provide signed compliance documentation.

How do living walls and green roofs survive extreme winter freezing?

We select hardy, regional alpine and sub-arctic species (including Sedum kamtschaticum, Sempervivum, and native evergreen ferns) that go dormant during winter and re-emerge vibrant in spring. Our patented root-cell media also provides thermal buffer protection down to -25°F (-31°C).

What municipal tax incentives, grants, and LEED credits are available?

Most metropolitan regions offer direct stormwater fee abatements, clean air grants, and property tax credits covering up to $15-$25 per square foot. Furthermore, green roofs earn significant points toward LEED v4.1 Platinum certification in categories including Sustainable Sites, Water Efficiency, and Energy & Atmosphere.

How much ongoing maintenance is required after installation?

With our automated IoT sensor-driven irrigation systems, extensive green roofs require only 2 seasonal visits per year for weeding and nutrient top-off. Living walls utilize automated closed-loop fertigation systems. We also offer fully managed ongoing maintenance packages so property owners never have to worry.
