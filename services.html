Next-Gen Hydroponics

Closed-Loop Sensor Driven Precision Irrigation

Conserving up to 75% more water than conventional sprinkler systems.

Our proprietary living wall irrigation modules operate on a closed-loop recirculating sub-irrigation matrix. Buried capacitive sensors sample volumetric water content 60 times an hour, automatically withholding water if natural rain is forecast or delivering micro-doses of organic nutrients precisely when transpiration demands spike.

  • Satellite Weather Integration: Suspends cycles during rainfall
  • Pressure Regulated Drippers: Uniform moisture across 100 vertical feet
  • Early Warning Alerts: Instant SMS/email notifications for flow anomalies

Client Checklist

Is Your Property Ready for Living Architecture?

Before scheduling a full structural audit, check if your site meets these standard indicators:

Structural Blueprint or Construction Age: Building plans or structural records available to verify deck joist spacing.
Direct Sunlight Exposure: Minimum of 3 to 4 hours of natural indirect or direct light (or dedicated grow-light ceiling junction).
Water Supply Line: A dedicated cold water bib or mechanical room riser within 50 feet of the installation boundary.
Electrical Outlet: Standard 110V/220V GFCI circuit for the IoT smart controller and solenoid manifold.
