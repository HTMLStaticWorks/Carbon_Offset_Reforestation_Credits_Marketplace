Structural Precision

The 6-Layer Engineered Strata

Each GreenScape roof installation is a multi-barrier living system designed to safeguard your building for over 50 years.

06

Mature Pre-Cultivated Vegetation Mat

95% pre-rooted sedum and succulent blends that deliver immediate aesthetic coverage and microclimate cooling.

05

Engineered Lightweight Substrate Matrix

Expanded volcanic shale, porous pumice, and organic compost engineered to not degrade or compact over decades.

04

Permeable Geotextile Filter Fabric

Allows surplus moisture to freely permeate downward while preventing fine substrate particles from washing into drainage cells.

03

Honeycomb Drainage & Water Storage Trays

100% recycled HDPE cups that retain vital moisture for dry spells while channeling excessive storm surges straight to roof scuppers.

02

Heavy-Duty Puncture Protection Fleece

Non-woven needle-punched synthetic cushion that absorbs structural deck shifting and guards against mechanical impacts.

01

Anti-Root Penetration & Waterproofing Membrane

Commercial-grade elastomeric membrane infused with non-toxic root deterrents, guaranteed waterproof for 30+ years.
