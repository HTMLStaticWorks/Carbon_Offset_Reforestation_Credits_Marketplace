Consultation Help

Frequently Asked Inquiries

Have questions before reaching out? Here are answers to our most common questions.

Is the preliminary consultation and budget estimation free?

Yes, 100% free! Our 30-minute initial video consultation and preliminary budget estimate carry zero obligation. If you proceed with detailed engineering drawings, a deposit is applied toward your final installation invoice.

What information should I prepare before our initial meeting?

It is helpful to have approximate rooftop or wall square footage, photos of the current space, building construction age, and whether an architectural blueprint or CAD file is available. If you don't have blueprints, our team can perform on-site laser measurements.

Do you work with general contractors and commercial developers on RFPs?

Yes. Over 60% of our installations are collaborative partnerships with commercial general contractors, landscape architects, and LEED consultants. We provide CSI MasterFormat specifications (Division 07 55 63 - Vegetated Protected Membrane Roofing) and full submittal packages.
